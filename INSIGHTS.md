# Insights

all the numbers here come from the cleaned data, 798 human journeys across 796 matches from feb 10 to 14.

## 1. bots do almost all the killing and they do it early

Going through the death events there was barely any pvp. 403 of the 445 human deaths (91%) are to bots, 39 are to the storm and only 3 are to other players. The median bot death happens 4.3 minutes in while the median run lasts about 6 minutes, so most players who die go down early.

The metrics this moves are early death rate (deaths before minute 5) and extraction rate, plus pvp encounters per match if pvp is meant to be part of the loop. I would look at bot density around the spots where deaths cluster (see 2) and ease it near landing areas so players get a few minutes to gear up, and if pvp matters then loot and extraction routes need to overlap more so humans cross paths at all. Right now bots shape a run more than anything else on the map, so where bots stand is level design as much as where the walls are.


## 2. most of the map barely gets used

Splitting ambrose valley into a 16x16 grid, the busiest 25 squares (10% of the grid) hold 60% of all human movement and only 129 of 256 squares get visited at all, some of the empty ones are water so that overstates it a bit. Deaths are tighter still, 5 squares hold a third of all deaths (103 of 316), two on the left side at mid height and two right in the middle. Lockdown is even more packed with 65% of movement in 10% of the grid.

The metrics here are map coverage and deaths per area. Those 5 death squares need a look in playback to see if they are chokepoints or bot camps, and adding cover or a second route through them should spread deaths out. The unvisited areas either need a reason to go there like better loot or they are space the map could lose, because space nobody visits is design time players never see and a few squares killing a third of players feels like a trap more than a fight.


## 3. the storm kills three times more people on lockdown

Lockdown is the small close quarters map so i expected the storm to matter less there, its the opposite. 10% of lockdown runs end in a storm death (17 of 171) against 3% on ambrose valley (17 of 568), and the storm is 17% of all deaths on lockdown but only 5% on ambrose valley. Lockdown also has the longest matches with a 7.1 minute median against 5.9, and storm deaths happen late, around minute 12. Grand rift shows a similar rate but its only 5 deaths so i wouldnt read much into it.

The metrics are storm death rate and time to extract on lockdown. I would check how far players are from the nearest extraction at minute 10 to 12 and wether the routes to it get blocked, then try adding an extraction point or shortening the path near where storm deaths cluster. Dying to the storm feels like the maps fault more than a lost fight, and on a small map players expect a way out to always be close.
